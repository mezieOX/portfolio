FAQ

Common questions answered

Quick answers about how I work, what I build, and how we can collaborate.

What does your development process look like?

I start by clarifying goals, users, and constraints—then break work into small shippable slices. From there I prototype UI, integrate APIs, polish responsive behavior, and hand off with clear docs so the team can maintain what ships.

What tools do you use every day?

Collaboration lives in Slack, Google Meet, and Zoom. Code happens in VS Code and Cursor, with GitHub for reviews and CI. Design handoff is in Figma; planning and docs sit in Notion and Jira; APIs get exercised in Postman.

Do you work on web, mobile, or both?

Both—plus AI. I ship responsive web apps with React, Next.js, TypeScript, and AI features, and mobile products with React Native—often sharing components, design tokens, and API layers across platforms.

Are you available for freelance or contract work?

Yes—open to freelance, contract, and select full-time collaborations. Share a short brief (timeline, stack, and goals) and I’ll reply with fit, approach, and next steps.

How do you measure success on a project?

Clear outcomes: smoother UX, faster load and interaction, fewer bugs in production, and features that land on schedule. I track performance, accessibility, and feedback from users and stakeholders—not just “looks done.”

How can we start working together?

Email me at meziepage@gmail.com or use the contact form. Include context on the product, preferred stack, and timeline— I’ll come back with questions and a proposed plan.
